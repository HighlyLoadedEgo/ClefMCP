# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful and constructive. We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/):

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

## Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behavior and take fair corrective action in response to unacceptable behavior, including removal of comments, commits, code, and banning of participants.

## Scope

This applies within project spaces (issues, PRs, discussions) and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report abusive behavior to the maintainers via GitHub. All complaints are reviewed and handled with discretion.
